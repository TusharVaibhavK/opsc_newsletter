There are more than twenty mentored open source programs, on different calendars. Between them you can be in one almost all year. The trick is to filter early by the things you can't change: eligibility, timing and whether you need a stipend.

## Answer four questions first

1. **Are you eligible?** Some programs are for students only (Summer of Bitcoin, OSRE, Open Mainframe). Outreachy is for people facing underrepresentation or systemic bias in tech where they live, and its initial application is a gate you pass before you can even see the projects. Read eligibility rules before investing time.
2. **When are you free?** GSoC coding runs roughly late May to August. Outreachy has May and December cohorts. LFX Mentorship has three terms a year. Students in Outreachy need 42 consecutive days free of classes and exams.
3. **Do you need a stipend?** 12 of the 22 programs listed here are paid. Djangonaut Space is unpaid but excellent mentoring; Hacktoberfest and 24 Pull Requests are events, not jobs.
4. **What do you want to work on?** Backend and cloud people should look hard at LFX (Go, Kubernetes, observability). Python backend people at Djangonaut Space. ML people at sktime and OSRE.

The [program finder](../../finder/) asks these questions and ranks programs for you.

## The big three

| Program | Who | Pay | When | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Summer of Code | 18+, students or open source beginners | Stipend, scaled by size and country | Orgs announced in February, proposals in March, coding May–August | Almost everyone; 184 orgs in 2026 |
| Outreachy | People facing underrepresentation in tech, per its rules | Paid internship | May and December cohorts | Eligible applicants; strong mentoring |
| LFX Mentorship | Open to all | Stipend | Three terms: Mar–May, Jun–Aug, Sep–Nov | Go, Kubernetes, cloud native |

## Stack programs on different calendars

Programs don't compete for your time if you plan around their calendars:

- **October–November:** contribute to your target orgs; join a Hacktoberfest event if it helps you get started.
- **January–February:** LFX Term 1 applications (late January to early February in 2026), Season of KDE, Outreachy May-cohort initial applications.
- **February–April:** GSoC org announcements, then proposals. European Summer of Code runs in two batches.
- **May onwards:** GSoC coding, LFX Term 2, summer fellowships (FOSSEE, C4GT).

The [timeline](../../timeline/) shows every window side by side, and the [calendar feed](../../deadlines.ics) puts the dated ones in your calendar.

## Read the fine print

- Dates marked **est.** on this site are last year's dates moved forward. They will shift; confirm on the official site.
- Pay is listed as the program describes it. “Varies” and “verify” mean exactly that.
- If a program says “students preferred” or “mainly India”, check the official rules before ruling yourself in or out.
